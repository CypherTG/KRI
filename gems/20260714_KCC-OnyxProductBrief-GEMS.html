01 Product Overview

Product Description
GEMS (Global Engagement Measuring System is an enterprise platform that helps organizations manage government relations, regulatory engagement, and stakeholder interactions in one centralized system. It allows users to schedule and track engagements, manage action items, monitor compliance, generate executive reports and dashboards, measure relationship performance through OKRs, and maintain a secure repository of regulatory and stakeholder information. GEMS improves collaboration, transparency, and strategic decision-making while strengthening relationships with regulators and other key stakeholders.
Current Process / What it Replaces
It replaces a manual Excel sheet that only covers an the classifications and also covers licenses.

02 The Problem

Current Situation
Government relations and industry stakeholder engagement activities are currently managed through spreadsheets, individual knowledge resulting in fragmented information, in-consistent follow ups. There is no centralized system to track engagements with regulators, clients, industry stakeholders, government agencies, host communities, or strategic partners, making it difficult to monitor commitments, measure relationship health, or demonstrate the value of government relations efforts. Reporting is largely manual, time-consuming, and often reactive, while important institutional knowledge can be lost when personnel change. This lack of visibility increases the risk of missed follow-ups, duplicated efforts, regulatory non-compliance, and missed business opportunities. Senior management also has limited access to real-time insights needed to make informed strategic decisions regarding stakeholder engagement and regulatory relationships.
Root Cause
The problem exists because government relations and stakeholder engagement are still managed through disconnected manual processes rather than a centralized digital system. Information is scattered across emails, spreadsheets, and individual staff records, making it difficult to track engagements, monitor commitments, measure performance, and provide management with real-time insights. This creates inefficiencies, increases compliance risks, and limits strategic decision-making.
Opportunity
GEMS transforms a traditionally reactive and manual function into a strategic, data-driven capability. Organizations gain a single source of truth for all stakeholder interactions, enabling better coordination, accountability, and collaboration across teams. Executives have real-time visibility into relationship health, regulatory engagements, outstanding commitments, compliance obligations, and emerging risks through intuitive dashboards and analytics. The platform improves decision-making, reduces regulatory and operational risks, preserves institutional knowledge, strengthens stakeholder relationships, and helps organizations identify new business opportunities. Ultimately, GEMS enables organizations to engage the right stakeholders at the right time, demonstrate measurable value from government relations activities, and support sustainable business growth through stronger regulatory and strategic partnerships.

03 Goals & Success

Main Goal
To provide organizations with a centralized, intelligent platform for managing government relations, stakeholder engagement, regulatory compliance, and strategic partnerships, enabling better decision-making, stronger accountability, and measurable business outcomes. This is done through being able to classify stakeholders, monitor licenses and permits, schedule meetings and track actions.
Objectives
1. Enable planning, scheduling, tracking, and documentation of meetings, visits, correspondence, and engagements with regulators and key stakeholders.
2. Monitor regulatory commitments, action items, approvals, permits, and compliance obligations with automated reminders and alerts.
3. Measure the effectiveness of stakeholder relationships using customizable OKRs, engagement scores, and performance dashboards.
4. Provide executives with real-time analytics and reports to support strategic decision-making and risk management. Improve accountability by assigning ownership, tracking progress, and maintaining complete audit trails for all engagements and commitments
5. Preserve institutional knowledge through a centralized repository of stakeholder profiles, engagement history, regulatory documents, and meeting records.
6. Reduce manual reporting, eliminate fragmented record-keeping, and improve operational efficiency through workflow automation and standardized processes.
7. Strengthen collaboration across Government Relations, Business Development, Legal, Projects, HSE, and Executive Management through shared workflows and controlled access. Identify business opportunities, regulatory trends, and stakeholder risks early through data-driven insights and engagement intelligence.
Success Definition
Success is achieved when GEMS is fully adopted as the single source of truth for government relations and stakeholder management, with all engagements, commitments, and compliance activities managed through the platform. Executives can monitor performance through real-time dashboards, teams collaborate effectively, manual processes are significantly reduced, regulatory risks are proactively managed, and measurable improvements are seen in stakeholder engagement, compliance, and decision-making
